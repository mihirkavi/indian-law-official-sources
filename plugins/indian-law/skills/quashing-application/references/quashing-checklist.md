# Analysis and quality checklist — quashing applications

Work from the record; every point below either becomes a ground, a placeholder, or a note to the advocate.

## Stage and relief

- Investigation pending: quash FIR; interim relief is usually protection from coercive action and/or a direction not to file a final report without leave. A blanket stay of investigation is exceptional — check current Supreme Court guidance on interim orders in quashing petitions before asking for it.
- Charge-sheet filed: quash FIR and the RCC/charge-sheet; interim stay of the trial proceedings (and execution of any warrants shown in the order sheet). Quashing remains available after a charge-sheet.
- Read the order sheet: summons, bailable/non-bailable warrants, next date, any discharge application.

## Per-applicant assessment

- Tabulate each accused: specific acts alleged, dates, place, words, injuries, medical evidence, who saw it. Separate the husband from relatives; note relatives living separately, unmarried siblings, aged parents (context, not immunity).
- Do not claim "no particulars" where the FIR gives them. State honest prospects per applicant to the advocate.

## Offences (check the official text each time)

- BNS 85 / s.86 cruelty: (a) wilful conduct likely to drive to suicide or cause grave injury/danger; (b) harassment to coerce an unlawful demand for property/valuable security. Check who demanded what, when.
- BNS 115(2) hurt; 126/127 wrongful restraint/confinement; 351 criminal intimidation (threat of injury with intent to cause alarm); 352 intentional insult intending/knowing provocation to break peace. Missing words, intent or threat are frequent gaps.
- Classification, punishment and limitation from the BNSS First Schedule and ss.514–519 (continuing offence s.518; extension s.519). Limitation is computed to the date of FIR/complaint (s.514 Explanation).

## Temporal applicability

- BNS and BNSS commenced 01/07/2024. For acts wholly before that date, s.358(2) BNS saves IPC liability; registration/charge-sheet under BNS shows non-application of mind. Caution: s.358(3) deems things done under the IPC to be done under BNS, and a wrong section is often treated as curable — use as a supporting ground, not a stand-alone one, unless verified authority supports more.
- Compare the FIR's "date of occurrence" column with its narrative.

## Record contradictions and context

- Earlier written complaints, counselling/Bharosa cell records, legal notices, maintenance/RCR/divorce/DV pleadings: compare dates, amounts, demands, assault descriptions and persons named. Silence about later allegations in an earlier document is significant.
- Witnesses: relatives only? hearsay? statements copied from the FIR? independent witness or medical record absent?
- Sequence: complaint after counselling failure, after the husband's legal steps, or long after leaving the matrimonial home; blank "reasons for delay" column.
- Jurisdiction: the court where the wife takes shelter may have jurisdiction over cruelty; do not lead with territorial objections without verified authority.
- Sensitive facts in the record that may hurt the applicants (e.g. allegations of another marriage) — flag to the advocate; do not omit silently from the analysis.

## Final quality check (before delivery)

- Parties, ages, relationships and addresses consistent across cause title, facts and prayers; discrepancies between record documents flagged.
- FIR number/date/police station/district, charge-sheet and RCC details consistent everywhere; no reference to a charge-sheet if investigation is pending (and vice versa).
- Every invoked section listed in the title, facts, grounds and prayers.
- Grounds detailed, non-repetitive, each tied to the record; individual allegations against each applicant considered.
- Proceeds under Section 528 BNSS; prayer matches the stage and relief legally available.
- Annexure labels match body and prayers; translation undertaking accurate.
- Every authority verified (or quoted "as reproduced in" an official judgment); adverse authorities noted separately.
- Solemn affirmation: no invented deponent or authorisation; paragraph range correct.
- Builder report: zero unexpected placeholders; page 1 renders with the boxed cause title; Times New Roman 14 pt, double spacing, A4 and margins intact.

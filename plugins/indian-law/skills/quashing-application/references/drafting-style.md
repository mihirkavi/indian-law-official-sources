# House drafting style — APL under Section 528 BNSS

Distilled from the advocate's own filed-format sample (client details removed). Follow the structure and phrasing; correct grammar and statute names silently (for example "Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita", "undertake", "plenitude").

## Format (carried by the template)

A4; margins top 2", left 2", bottom 1", right 1"; Times New Roman 14 pt throughout; double line spacing; justified body; page number at top centre in the header; no colours or modern headings. Do not condense formatting to save pages.

## Sequence

1. Heading (bold, underlined, centred): `IN THE HON’BLE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY, NAGPUR BENCH, NAGPUR` (change the bench only on instruction).
2. `CRIMINAL APPLICATION (APL) NO.             /20__`
3. Boxed cause title: left column `APPLICANTS` / `NON-APPLICANTS` (bold, underlined), right column numbered parties — **Name S/o / W/o / D/o Father-or-Husband Surname,** Aged about __ years, Occ: __, then address lines, then `…..(Relationship)` set to the right. Common addresses as "2 to 4 R/o …". Merged row `:: VERSUS ::`. Non-applicant 1 is "State of Maharashtra, Through Police Station Officer, <PS>, Dist. <District>."; non-applicant 2 the complainant with `…..(Complainant)`.
4. Title lines (label bold-underlined): `APPLICATION U/S 528 OF BHARATIYA NAGARIK SURAKSHA SANHITA, 2023`; `FIR No: <no>/<year>, dated <dd/mm/yyyy>, registered with Police Station <PS>, Dist. <District>.`; if charge-sheeted, `RCC NO. <no> pending on the file of learned Judicial Magistrate First Class, <place> arising out of Charge sheet no. <no>, dated <date>.`; `OFFENCES PUNISHABLE U/S: Section …, Section … of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.`
5. Opening: "The Applicants above-named most humbly and respectfully beg to submit as under: -"
6. Numbered facts (1., 2., …), typically:
   - "The Applicants are peace loving and law-abiding citizens of India, residing at the above-mentioned addresses in the title clause. The applicant no 1 and non-applicant no 2 got married on … as per … . The Applicant No. 2 is … of the non-applicant no 2." Mention relatives who reside separately.
   - "By the present application, the applicants are seeking quashment of FIR … registered with the non-applicant no 1 Police Station, … for the offences punishable under … . The applicants are also seeking to quash criminal proceedings bearing RCC no. … pending on the file of learned Judicial Magistrate of First Class, … arising out of Charge sheet no. … filed on … ."
   - Background proceedings (complaints, counselling cell, notices, family-court cases) — only as the record shows.
   - "That, the complainant i.e. non-applicant no 2 had filed a written complaint to the non-applicant no 1 police station and therefore the FIR bearing no … was registered against the applicants. The copy of the said FIR dated … is annexed herewith as “ANNEXURE A”. Bare perusal of the said FIR would reveal that the Non-Applicant no 2 has alleged therein as under –" followed by an indented quoted English summary of the FIR, faithful to its attributions.
   - "It is most humbly submitted that the Non-Applicant no 1 has placed the Chargesheet bearing no. … before the learned Judicial Magistrate First Class, … . The said case is registered as R.C.C. no … . The copy of the said Chargesheet … is filed herewith “ANNEXURE B”."
   - "It is most humbly submitted that the Applicants are falsely roped in the present case. Bare perusal of the FIR and Chargesheet would reveal that prima facie, no case is made out against the applicants for the alleged offences. Hence, the applicants are seeking to quash an afore said FIR and Chargesheet, on the following grounds -"
7. `GROUNDS` (bold, underlined) then lettered grounds A), B), C) … in full paragraphs. Typical openings: "Bare perusal of the FIR would reveal that …", "It is submitted that …", "It is further pertinent to note here that …", "That, in a judgment in the case of … the Hon’ble Apex Court …". Close each ground: "Hence, on this count also, the said FIR and proceedings arising out of the same deserve to be quashed and set aside." Case names bold-underlined. Final ground: conduct of the complainant, no prima facie case, abuse of process, "parameters formulated by the Hon’ble Apex Court in the matter of State of Haryana vs. Bhajan Lal, 1992 Supp (1) SCC 335, under Section 528 of the BNSS".
8. Concluding numbered paragraphs: no other efficacious remedy / inherent powers under Section 528 BNSS; undertaking to file translations (state that the papers are in Marathi when they are); previous-proceedings statement — only after confirmation.
9. "Hence, this Application." (indented).
10. `PRAYER:-  It is, therefore, most humbly prayed that this Hon'ble Court may kindly be pleased to:-` then lettered prayers: quash FIR (naming every applicant "Applicant no 1) … , Applicant no 2) …"); quash RCC/charge-sheet; interim relief appropriate to the stage; "Grant ad-interim relief in terms of prayer clause …;"; "Allow the application with costs;"; "Be pleased to grant such other relief and pass such order as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper in the facts and circumstances of the case, in the interest of justice."
11. `NAGPUR … Counsel For Applicants` / `DATE- … Adv. <name>`.
12. `SOLEMN AFFIRMATION`: "I, …, Aged about … years, R/o …, the applicant no … [for himself/herself and on behalf of applicant no … — only on instructions], do hereby take an oath and state on solemn affirmation that the contents of above all Paras 1 to … of the application are drafted by our counsel wholly as per our instructions and the same are read by me as also are read over and explained to me in vernacular language and which I have fully understood and I say and submit that the same are true and correct to the best of my personal knowledge, belief and information. That the documents placed on record are the true copies of the originals thereof. Hence sworn, verified and signed on this … at Nagpur." then `DEPONENT` (right), `I KNOW AND IDENTIFY THE DEPONENT`, `ADVOCATE`.

## Care points

- Para count in the affirmation must match the numbered paragraphs (the builder fills `{last_para}`).
- Annexure letters in the body, prayers and annexure list must match.
- Do not copy citations from an earlier draft without re-verifying; a reference sample contained a citation in which a 2020 decision was said to rely on a December 2024 judgment.
- Quote judgments only with verified wording; otherwise state the proposition without quotation marks.

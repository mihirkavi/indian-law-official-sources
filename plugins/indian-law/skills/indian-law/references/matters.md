# Maharashtra matters

Use [research.md](research.md) for law and citations, [access.md](access.md) for barriers, and [sources.json](sources.json) for official routes. Work with minimal facts needed for the task; use synthetic examples instead of real identifiers for testing.

## Drafting

Support notices, plaints, written statements, interim applications, bail applications, replies, matrimonial/maintenance/domestic-violence pleadings, and submissions. Confirm forum, party capacity, procedural stage, facts/dates, relief, existing proceedings, and governing law. A general outline can proceed before those facts are known; do not present it as ready to file.

Civil: verify jurisdiction, pecuniary/territorial competence, cause of action, limitation, standing, court fees and local procedure as relevant. Criminal: distinguish regular/anticipatory/default bail and other remedies; confirm pending procedural regime, allegations, applicable special statutes and restrictions. Family/domestic: identify the intended statutory route/forum and concurrent proceedings; do not infer personal law from names, language, gender assumptions, or a generic “maintenance” request.

Keep party allegations as allegations. Separate supplied facts, assumptions requiring confirmation, and missing instructions. Use conspicuous `[FACT REQUIRED: ...]` or requested-language equivalents instead of inventing dates/income/children/grounds. Cite only verified authority. Confirm court rules, formatting and filing language from the relevant official court; say when not checked. Use [pleading.md](../assets/pleading.md) for a reviewable draft and list documents/facts the lawyer must confirm.

Limitation: collect accrual/service dates, proceedings/remedy, exclusions, holidays and condonation issues. Show the basis, calculation and assumptions with official support; a hearing date is not an automatic filing deadline. No filing, sending, purchase, sworn verification, or representation of lawyer approval is authorized by generating a draft.

## Case status and hearing preparation

Resolve court/bench, CNR or case type-number-year/diary number, and parties. Names alone may identify multiple matters; obtain disambiguation before stating a person's status. Use `ecourts-services` for relevant district/session services, `bombay-hc` for High Court sources and `sci-status` for Supreme Court matters. Discover current official court-specific links rather than guessing query parameters.

Read status, most recent available order, cause list and registry notice as relevant. Report exact identity, observed status, listing date and source update/retrieval timestamp separately. Do not interpret “disposed” as the relief granted without the operative order. Conflicting sources remain visible; explain the latest relevant order and unresolved discrepancy rather than merging them into invented certainty. A downloaded public order is not necessarily a certified copy.

If CAPTCHA/login/technical failure blocks access, report what was and was not checked. A user-provided screenshot/PDF is a supplied record with its own date; do not upgrade it to live verified status. Build a hearing brief with recent orders, outstanding directions, relevant documents and unresolved facts only from inspected evidence.

## Property records

Ask for state, district, taluka, village, parcel/survey/gat/CTS number, property category and period. Use `mh-land` for preliminary revenue records, `mh-signed-land` for specifically requested signed records, `mh-archives` for available historical documents, and `mh-registration` for registered deeds/indexes. Inspect official service links and access state before use. A public research request does not automatically require the signed-download login.

Keep 7/12, 8A, property card, mutations, maps, deeds, indexes and encumbrance-related checks separate. Record document dates, identifiers, parties, areas, claimed interests, certified/signed status and verification result. Compare changes chronologically; flag missing conveyances, inconsistent parcel identifiers, boundaries/areas and periods not covered. Do not assert the legal effect of a mutation or a liability search without applicable official legislation/judgments.

Do not certify ownership/marketable title or absence of encumbrances from an empty search or a revenue entry. A registration index is not the full deed; a digitally signed revenue record is not itself a complete title chain. Identify which official copies or further searches are needed for the intended purpose; fees/authentication are service-specific and require actual observation. Avoid broad publication of owner names or identifiers.

Other states: follow the DoLR directory and the responsible Revenue/Registration Department, verify current provenance and the record type, and add a source-ledger entry. Unresolved/disabled candidates in the registry cannot be queried as if verified endpoints.

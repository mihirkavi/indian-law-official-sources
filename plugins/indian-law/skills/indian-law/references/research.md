# Official legal research

Use [sources.json](sources.json) to find relevant issuers. Useful IDs: `india-code`, `central-gazette`, `mha`, `mh-law`, `mh-gazette`, `mh-home`, `sci`, `scr`, `verdict-finder`, `bombay-hc`, and `judges-library`. If a source is blocked, apply [access.md](access.md).

## Frame the question

Identify the material issue, territorial/forum jurisdiction, factual dates and procedural posture, requested remedy, and language. Maharashtra is the default research priority, not a reason to discard applicable central legislation or Supreme Court authority. Avoid guessing personal law, court competence, legal regime, or missing dates. Resolve facts that change the route; proceed with source discovery where independent.

Search by exact Act title/year/number, case name/date/number, notification number and issuer, and relevant legal synonyms. Use domain-scoped search where helpful, but inspect publisher identity and the original document. An index hit, official press summary, search result, or library citation is not the original holding or statutory text. Record unsuccessful searches so an authority can remain unverified without claiming non-existence.

## Statutes and temporal application

1. Retrieve relevant provision and the Act identity; check territorial and subject-matter application. Statutory indexes may omit recent changes.
2. Identify enactment separately from commencement, including section-specific exceptions. Obtain the actual Gazette/issuer notification and amendment text; bills and draft rules are not enacted law.
3. For historical questions, reconstruct the provision applicable on the material date, including amendment effective dates, repeal/savings, transition rules, and any relevant judicial construction. For current questions, state the date and bounded sources searched for subsequent changes.
4. Distinguish statute/rule/notification/circular and verify the issuing power where relevant; disclose apparent conflicts rather than arbitrarily selecting a source.

For IPC/CrPC/Evidence Act versus BNS/BNSS/BSA, ask for offence date, investigation/trial/appeal commencement and pending status as relevant. Check each code's distinct commencement and savings provisions; a pending procedure question is not answered by offence date alone. Never map old and new section numbers as if identical. Do not infer present commencement solely from the original commencement notification.

Report a concise timeline: instrument, section/change, issue/publication date, effective date, territorial scope, official original URL, and uncertainty. Quote only inspected text; otherwise paraphrase with an exact citation and verified pinpoint.

## Judgments and precedent

Retrieve the original official judgment/order. Verify case identity, decision date, bench, strength, disposition, and any separate opinions. A reporting/neutral citation should be checked against the document. Identify issues, facts material to the decision, holding, reasoning, observations, party submissions, and operative relief separately.

Use the judgment's printed paragraph numbers. For unnumbered text, cite printed PDF pages/sections and distinguish viewer page indices; never turn OCR line numbers into paragraph citations. Read context around a proposed pinpoint to ensure it supports the claim. AI summaries are not official headnotes.

For Maharashtra, evaluate Supreme Court and the relevant Bombay High Court authority with applicable binding/persuasive rules, bench strength, and jurisdiction. Search for adverse decisions and later treatment, including larger-bench rulings, stays, reviews, recalls, and legislative changes. A short search is not an exhaustive citator. “No adverse treatment found in [sources/date/search scope]” is narrower than “good law.”

If an authority or alleged universal proposition cannot be verified, withhold a substantive brief based on it. Offer verified alternative research only when useful and clearly separate it from the unverified case. Do not fabricate an answer to fill a requested format.

## Output and language

Use the [research memo](../assets/research-memo.md) or [judgment brief](../assets/judgment-brief.md) when appropriate; ordinary answers can be shorter. Lead with the answer, support material claims near the text with exact official links and pinpoints, then include a compact source ledger and material limitations. A row with an unverified document must not support a “verified” conclusion.

Respond in the requested language to the host's ability. Keep original citations, section numbers, case/Act identities, dates and untranslated legal terms where needed. Prefer verified official translations; label model-generated translations, flag uncertain terms, and provide the original excerpt when wording matters. Transliteration is not legal translation. Do not promise authoritative output in every language or compliance with filing-language rules without checking them.
